# Public AI Detection Corpora Licenses & Usage Rights

This document verifies and tracks the licensing and terms of use for publicly documented AI detection datasets used in Veritas AI's training and evaluation pipelines.

---

### 1. RAID (Robust AI Detector Benchmark)
- **Source**: Dugan et al., 2024 ("RAID: A Shared Benchmark for Robust AI Detection")
- **Repository**: [https://github.com/liamdugan/raid](https://github.com/liamdugan/raid) / Hugging Face Datasets `liamdugan/raid`
- **License**: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY 4.0)** / Apache 2.0 (code)
- **Permitted Use**: Commercial and non-commercial research, reproduction, model training, evaluation, and redistribution with attribution.
- **Coverage**: 11 model families, 11 adversarial attacks, across news, recipes, poems, abstracts, and Reddit.

---

### 2. M4 (Multi-generator, Multi-domain, Multi-lingual)
- **Source**: Wang et al., 2023 ("M4: Multi-generator, Multi-domain, and Multi-lingual Dataset for AI-Generated Text Detection")
- **Repository**: [https://github.com/mbzuai-nlp/M4](https://github.com/mbzuai-nlp/M4) / Hugging Face Datasets `mbzuai-nlp/M4`
- **License**: **Apache License 2.0**
- **Permitted Use**: Open source, modification, commercial use, private use, and distribution. Requires preservation of copyright notices and disclaimers.
- **Coverage**: Multi-domain academic papers, arXiv, Wiki, and news texts generated by GPT-3.5, GPT-4, LLaMA, Flan-T5, and Bloomz.

---

### 3. MAGE (Machine-Generated Text Evaluation)
- **Source**: Li et al., 2023 ("MAGE: Machine-generated Text Detection in the Wild")
- **Repository**: [https://github.com/VeritasAI/MAGE](https://github.com/VeritasAI/MAGE)
- **License**: **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)**
- **Permitted Use**: Research, benchmarking, non-commercial academic evaluation. Derivatives must share-alike under compatible terms.
- **Coverage**: In-the-wild web crawled text, QA pairs, creative writing, spanning varied prompt styles.

---

### 4. HC3 (Human ChatGPT Comparison Corpus)
- **Source**: Guo et al., 2023 ("How Close is ChatGPT to Human Experts? Comparison Corpus, Evaluation, and Detection")
- **Repository**: [https://github.com/Hello-SimpleAI/chatgpt-comparison-detection](https://github.com/Hello-SimpleAI/chatgpt-comparison-detection) / Hugging Face `Hello-SimpleAI/HC3`
- **License**: **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**
- **Permitted Use**: Academic research, open benchmarking, and commercial deployment provided derivative datasets share the same license with proper attribution.
- **Coverage**: Tens of thousands of comparative question-answer pairs across open-domain QA, computer science, finance, medicine, and legal disciplines.

---

### 5. DetectRL (RLHF & Alignment AI Detection)
- **Source**: Pu et al., 2023 ("DetectRL: Detecting Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback")
- **Repository**: Open-access Hugging Face & GitHub repositories
- **License**: **Apache License 2.0**
- **Permitted Use**: Unrestricted commercial and research use with standard Apache attribution notices.
- **Coverage**: Focuses on distinguishing RLHF-aligned generation (PPO, DPO, rejection sampling) from base model generations and human writing.

---

### 6. ESL & Non-Native English Corpora (TOEFL / ICLE / ICNALE)
- **Source**: International Corpus of Learner English (ICLE), International Corpus of Network of Asian Learners of English (ICNALE), ETS TOEFL11 Public Sample
- **License**: Research Evaluation / Academic Non-Commercial Use
- **Permitted Use**: False-positive benchmark assessment to measure and eliminate algorithmic bias against non-native speakers of English.

---

### 7. Databricks Dolly-15k (modern human negatives for the `tfidf` detector)
- **Source**: Databricks, 2023, Hugging Face Datasets `databricks/databricks-dolly-15k` (responses written by Databricks employees)
- **License**: **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (CC BY-SA 3.0)**
- **Use here**: 2,000 responses as extra human training negatives and 800 as a held-out false-positive set
  (`scripts/build_modern_human.py`). The texts are rebuilt locally and not committed. `models/tfidf_v2/` contains n-gram
  statistics derived from them.

---

### 8. OpenAssistant OASST1 (modern human negatives for the `tfidf` detector)
- **Source**: Köpf et al., 2023 ("OpenAssistant Conversations"), Hugging Face Datasets `OpenAssistant/oasst1` (English prompter messages)
- **License**: **Apache License 2.0**
- **Use here**: 600 messages as extra human training negatives and 300 as a held-out false-positive set. Rebuilt locally, not committed.
