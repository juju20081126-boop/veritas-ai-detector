## What this teardown says about *our* design (inferred)
1. Everyone that reports robustness trains on **attacked/humanized samples plus hard negatives** (GPTZero "Paraphraser Shield", Pangram active learning, DAMAGE oversampled humanizers). Zero-shot probability scores alone (Binoculars, Fast-DetectGPT) lose most of their power on humanized text (S4, S16).
2. Sentence/segment windows with aggregation (GPTZero, Turnitin, QuillBot, Pangram) are the common output design; our engine already does chunk pooling + sentence smoothing.
3. "AI-refined / mixed" is a first-class class at QuillBot, Pangram and Turnitin; evaluation must keep it as a separate slice (ARB, S2).
4. All vendors say short text is unreliable; our headline bucket is >= 150 words with <80 words reported separately.
5. Vendor accuracy numbers are self-reported and mostly on vendor benchmarks; the only like-for-like comparisons we can trust are ours (same locked test) or independent papers (S4, S14, S16). Black-box probing of commercial UIs is out of scope (ToS); official APIs with the user's key (e.g. Sapling's free tier) or hand-collected CSVs are the allowed routes.
