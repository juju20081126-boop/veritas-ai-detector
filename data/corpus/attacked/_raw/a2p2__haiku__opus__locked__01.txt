- **Problem resolution.** They can cure sickness, ailments, and hexes, and at higher levels can restore dead characters to life.

- Healing elixirs become the main healing source, but cost money and eventually get depleted.
- Rest stops become necessary regularly, which may allow enemies to regroup or cause time-sensitive missions to lapse.
- Other classes, such as druids, bards or paladins, can partially fill healing duty.
- Party members die more regularly, with permanent death possible when resurrection magic is unavailable.
- Players adopt more caution, choose avoidance over confrontation, and rely more on negotiation and stealth.

In current games, a priest isn't absolutely mandatory, but acts as a safety buffer. Removing that buffer means each misstep has greater costs, which some parties actually prefer as a greater test of skill.
